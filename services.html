Everything we do, in detail.

Six services, each retained in writing, each staffed by the people you meet on the first day. No tiered packages, no add-ons, no surprise line items.

Process

How a listing runs

Six stages over roughly nine days before we launch, then a weekly rhythm you can see and question.

Pre-listing walkthrough

We spend two hours in the property with a contractor, a photographer and a copywriter, and produce a prioritised list of everything that affects price. No charge, and no obligation to list with us.

Preparation

You decide what to fix. We coordinate the trades, hold the schedule, and manage the budget. Most of our clients spend between five and twenty thousand dollars here, and it returns roughly six to ten times that.

Positioning

Photography, film, floor plans and a written positioning document covering the buyer, the story and the two objections we expect to hear first.

Launch

Listing photography published, portals activated, our own database and broker network alerted on the same day. Your first open house is within ten days of launch.

Negotiation

We manage every offer, in writing, with a recommendation attached. You see the full picture — price, terms, contingencies and timing — and we tell you what we would accept.

Escrow and close

Our own operations desk coordinates inspections, appraisal, title and lender. You get a weekly status and a single point of contact until the keys are in your hand.
