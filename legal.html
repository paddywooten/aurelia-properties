Policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

What we collect

When you submit a form on this website we collect the name, email address, telephone number and message you provide, along with the page you submitted it from. We do not collect payment details, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry, to provide a valuation or consultation you have asked for, and — if you have opted in — to send the monthly Market Letter. We do not sell or share your details with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are retained for twenty-four months and then deleted. If you become a client, records are retained for the period required by California law, which is generally three years after a transaction closes.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you also have the right to know what we collect and to opt out of its sale — we do not sell personal information. Write to privacy@aureliaproperties.com and we will respond within thirty days.

Local storage

This website stores your shortlist of saved properties in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you clear your browser storage.

Cookies

We set no advertising or analytics cookies. The only browser storage this site uses is the local-storage shortlist described above, and the fonts and map tiles loaded from Google Fonts and OpenStreetMap, which are subject to those providers' own privacy policies.
