The difference

Preparation is not staging.

The gap between a listing that sits and a listing that sells is almost never the photographs. It is the weeks of preparation that happen before anyone sees the house.

Every Aurelia listing gets a pre-listing walkthrough before a single photograph is taken. We look for the deferred maintenance that buyers will price in as risk, the repairs that will show up at inspection, and the handful of changes that remove doubt rather than add decoration.

How we prepare a listing

We list less

Twelve to eighteen homes a year per agent. More attention per listing than a volume brokerage can give.

We prepare more

Nine days of work before launch, on average, focused on the things buyers discount hardest.

We report honestly

Weekly written reporting on showings, feedback and market movement — including when to change course.

We close carefully

Escrow managed by our own operations desk. Thirty-one days average, with no surprises at the end.
